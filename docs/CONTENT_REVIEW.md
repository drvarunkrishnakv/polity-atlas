  Article membership also follows the headings of Part III in the 2024 text.
  Includes Article 21A within the freedom category. Supporting and omitted
  provisions are presented separately; they are not additional rights categories.

- Constitution: official Legislative Department 2024 English PDF, through the 106th
  Amendment. Part III and relevant Article 226 / 359 text. Do not infer later legal
  developments from this edition alone.
- Article 31C: the printed expanded wording must be read with the Minerva Mills
  invalidation footnote. The sample describes protection for Article 39(b)/(c),
  rather than claiming protection for every Directive Principle.
- Puttaswamy: official Supreme Court PDF dated 24 August 2017. Court's order and
  Chandrachud J. paragraph 325. Maneka Gandhi is supported by paragraph 23 of that
  later judgment (PDF pp. 22–23), explicitly identified as a later discussion.

  contribution holdings are kept distinct. The event node is dated, not live news.

  on Indian Kanoon. This supports the basic-structure limitation on Ninth Schedule
  insertions on or after 24 April 1973; the source is not presented as an official host.
